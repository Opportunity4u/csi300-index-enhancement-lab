# Internship research submission

## Status

**Completed individual technical research project.**

This repository is the reproducible code component of a TradingBase.AI internship submission on CSI 300 index enhancement. The completed scope covers:

- price-only factor engineering and cross-sectional factor diagnostics;
- leakage-controlled walk-forward Ridge forecasts;
- benchmark-aware active portfolio construction;
- delayed execution, drifted holdings, turnover limits, no-trade bands, and transaction costs;
- expanding-window and rolling-window out-of-sample evaluation;
- automated timing and portfolio-invariant tests;
- paper-account order generation and forward monitoring.

## Submitted separately

The final English presentation, five-minute speaking script, and formatted research report are submitted as direct attachments. They are kept outside this repository so the repository remains lightweight and code-focused.

Project completion means that the research question, implementation, tests, documented limitations, and deliverables have been completed. It does not mean the strategy is approved for live capital. See [DATA_NOTICE.md](../DATA_NOTICE.md) and [DISCLAIMER.md](../DISCLAIMER.md).
